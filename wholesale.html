FAQ

Wholesale questions, answered.

What's the typical price range?+

FOB Shenzhen prices range from $4.50 to $9.00 per pair depending on material, complexity, and quantity. DDP prices for US/EU include shipping, customs, and duties →typically 2.5→.5× the FOB price. We send exact pricing in your quote within 24 hours →usually much faster.

How does sampling work?+

Custom samples: 7 days, $80–$150 per sample (refundable against bulk order over 1,000 pairs). Free for orders 1,000+ pairs. We offer 2 free revision rounds. White-label samples: 3 days, $30 per pair.

What about quality control and defects?+

Every order goes through a pre-shipment AQL 2.5 inspection (industry standard for soft goods). Defect rate is typically below 1.5%. We retain batch samples for 12 months. If you receive a defective pair, we replace it at no cost or credit your next order.

Do you handle shipping and customs?+

Yes. We offer EXW, FOB Shenzhen, CIF, and DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) to US, EU, UK, AU, and CA. DDP means the price you see is the price you pay →we handle customs, duties, and final-mile delivery. Typical transit: 15–25 days by sea, 5→ days by air.

Can you help with the design if we don't have one?+

Absolutely. Our ODM service includes 3 original concepts designed in-house, complete with tech-packs, material specs, and 3D mockups. Once you sign off and pay in full, you own 100% of the IP. Exclusive SKU protection is available for an additional fee.

What payment terms do you offer?+

30% T/T deposit upon order confirmation, 70% balance before shipment. For established clients (3+ orders), we can offer Net-30 after a credit check. We accept USD, EUR, and RMB via T/T or Alibaba Trade Assurance.

Can I visit the factory?+

Absolutely →and we recommend it. We're about 1 hour from Ningbo Lishe International Airport (NGB), with high-speed rail from Shanghai in 1.5 hours. We can arrange airport pickup, factory tour, showroom visit, and even same-day sample review. Just mention the visit in your quote request and we'll send an invite.
