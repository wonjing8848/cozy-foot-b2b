Why seasonal at Cozy Foot

Built for buyers who plan in seasons.

Seasonal programs have their own rhythm — sample in summer, ship by mid-November, restock in January. Our workshop is built around that cycle.

📅

Seasonal calendar locked

Sample 90 days before peak. Production 60 days before. We open Q4 slots every July and protect capacity for committed buyers.

🎨

120+ motif library

From pumpkin to reindeer. Mix and match across collections, or commission a fully custom design. Both turnkey and bespoke options.

📦

Gift-set ready

Custom gift box, ribbon, hangtag, care card, polybag. We handle the assembly so your slippers arrive shelf-ready or Amazon-FBA-ready.

🚢

DDP before the holiday

DDP shipping to US, EU, UK, AU, CA. Most orders arrive 15–25 days before the peak buying date — buffer built in.

📋

Compliance & safety

OEKO-TEX Standard 100, BSCI, Sedex, Prop 65, CE/CPC, FDA-approved materials. All documentation provided before shipment.

🔁

Restock friendly

Repeat orders cost less and ship faster. We retain your design files and color specs for 12+ months for quick reorders.
